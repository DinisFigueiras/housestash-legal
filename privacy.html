1. WHAT INFORMATION DO WE COLLECT?

Information stored only on your device

In Short: your board never reaches us.

The links you add (title, web address, price, room, whether you bought it, the address of its picture and the date you saved it) and your currency setting are stored locally on your device. We have no access to them. If you uninstall the app or use Delete all my data, this information is removed from your device.

Requests to the websites of the links you save

In Short: your phone contacts the shop, not us.

  • Reading the preview picture. When you save a link, the app requests the web page you entered, directly from your phone, to look for its product picture. This is the same request a browser makes when you open the link.
  • Showing the picture. The picture is displayed by loading it from the address the page provides (or the image address you typed). It is not copied to our servers because we have none.
  • The website you contact receives the information any web request carries, such as your IP address and your device and app type, and handles it under its own privacy policy. We do not receive or see these requests.

Backups you create

When you choose Export backup, the app writes a file containing your links and opens your device’s share sheet. The file goes only where you choose to save or send it, and anyone who has it can read your links. When you choose Restore from backup, the app reads the file you select on your device. Nothing is sent to us.

Device permissions

Nestboard does not ask for access to your camera, photo library, contacts, microphone or location, and it does not use an advertising identifier. Saving and picking backup files use your device’s own file and share screens.

Support requests

If you email us, we receive your email address and what you write, and we use them only to reply to you.

Sensitive information

We do not process sensitive personal information.
