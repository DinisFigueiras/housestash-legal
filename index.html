Frequently asked questions

Where are my links stored?

Only on your phone. Nestboard has no accounts and no cloud sync, and we cannot see your board. If you lose or reset your phone, your links are lost unless you made a backup. Go to Settings → Export backup now and then and keep the file somewhere safe (Files, iCloud Drive, Google Drive, email).

How do I move my links to a new phone?

On the old phone open Settings → Export backup and save the file. On the new phone install Nestboard, open Settings → Restore from backup and pick the file. Links that are already on the board are kept as they are and only the missing ones are added.

Why does a link have no picture?

When you save a link, Nestboard looks for the product picture on the page you entered. Some shops do not share it, so the card shows “No picture yet”. You can add one yourself: edit the link and paste an image address into Image URL (long-press the product photo in your browser and copy the image address).

Does changing the currency convert my prices?

No. Settings → Currency only changes the symbol shown next to your prices. If you switch from euros to dollars, a price of 100 stays 100. Nothing is converted.

Why do I see the shop’s page or picture load when I add a link?

To find the product picture, the app asks the shop’s website for the page you entered, directly from your phone, just like opening the link in a browser. We do not receive anything. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Does Nestboard work offline?

You can open your board, search, filter and edit without a connection. Product pictures are loaded from the shops’ websites, so they may need a connection to appear.

How do I delete my data?

Open Settings → Delete all my data to remove every link from your phone. Uninstalling the app also removes it. There is nothing to delete on our side because we never receive your links. Backup files you saved elsewhere are yours to delete.
