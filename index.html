Frequently asked questions

Where are my links stored?

Only on your phone. Nestboard has no accounts and no cloud sync, and we cannot see your board. If you lose or reset your phone, your links are lost unless you made a backup. Go to Settings → Export backup now and then and keep the file somewhere safe (Files, iCloud Drive, Google Drive, email).

How do I move my links to a new phone?

On the old phone open Settings → Export backup and save the file. On the new phone install Nestboard, open Settings → Restore from backup and pick the file. Links that are already on the board are kept as they are and only the missing ones are added. Pictures found on shop websites come back with their links; photos you took or picked on the old phone are not in the backup, so add them again if you need them.

Why does a link have no picture?

When you save a link, Nestboard looks for the product picture on the page you entered; the card says “Finding the picture…” while it looks. Some shops do not share it, so the card then shows “No picture yet”. You can add one yourself: edit the link and tap Add a photo (from your photo library) or Take a photo, or tap Paste a picture address instead and paste the address of the product photo (long-press it in your browser and copy the image address).

Does changing the currency convert my prices?

No. Settings → Currency only changes the symbol shown next to your prices. If you switch from euros to dollars, a price of 100 stays 100. Nothing is converted.

Why do I see the shop’s page or picture load when I add a link?

To find the product picture, the app asks the shop’s website for the page you entered, directly from your phone, just like opening the link in a browser. We do not receive anything. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Does Nestboard work offline?

Yes. You can open your board, search, filter and edit without a connection. Pictures are kept on your phone once they have been found, so they show offline too. Links saved with an older version of the app may still load their picture from the shop and need a connection.

Does Nestboard need my camera or photos?

Only if you want it to. When you tap Take a photo or Add a photo on a link, your phone asks for permission. The photo stays on your phone as that link’s picture and is never uploaded. If you say no, you can still paste a picture address. You can change this any time in your phone’s settings.

Can I see a picture bigger?

Yes. Open a link and tap its picture. Pinch or double-tap to zoom, and drag to move around. Double-tap again to go back.

How do I delete my data?

Open Settings → Delete all my data to remove every link, picture and photo from your phone. Uninstalling the app also removes it. There is nothing to delete on our side because we never receive your links. Backup files you saved elsewhere are yours to delete.
